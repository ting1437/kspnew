About us

Syarikat Perniagaan Kai Seng Sdn Bhd

A wholesaler of disposable food packaging that also prints it. We help small businesses own the packaging they hand over — in quantities a small business can actually afford.

Our story

We kept hearing the same question.

Shop owners came in for plain cups and bags, then asked the same thing at the counter: can I have my name on this? The usual answer in this trade is yes — if you order tens of thousands. For a new café or a home-based seller, that is not an answer.

So we brought printing in-house and set the minimum at one carton. Now the same visit covers all of it: choose the packaging, sort out the artwork, print a test batch, and print the run.

We are still a family wholesaler in Cheras, talking to shop owners in plain words. Nothing about that has changed.
